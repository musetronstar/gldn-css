The divine proportional semantic CSS system

Every proportion on this page derives from one constant: the golden ratio phi. Resize the window and watch the splits hold their ratio.

see the golden splits

golden splits

The fixed split deducts each column's golden share of the row gutter from its basis, so the columns plus the gutter fill the container in exact proportion; the fluid split grows the columns by weights phi and 1 instead. Both hold the ratio at any width. The measured ratios below are computed live from the rendered boxes.

the golden division cascade

Dividing the remainder by phi again and again yields the full proportional scale phi^-1 .. phi^-10, exposed as the custom properties --gldn-phi-1 through --gldn-phi-10.

phi^-1 = 61.803%
phi^-2 = 38.197%
phi^-3 = 23.607%
phi^-4 = 14.590%
phi^-5 = 9.017%
phi^-6 = 5.573%

typography on the golden scale

Each size step multiplies or divides by phi, and prose keeps a golden line height of phi itself.
